A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired: Malachar, Lord of the Ashen Court, keeper of the Ember Tithe. His tiredness lives in the deflections and the rhythm, never in a plain statement that he is weary.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (about 100 words, under the 600-character gate, byte-identical on repeated runs).
